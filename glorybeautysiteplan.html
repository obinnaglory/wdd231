GloryBeauty Palace – Site Plan

1. Site Name

GloryBeauty Palace

The name reflects elegance and beauty, aligning with the brand’s mission of offering stylish hair accessories.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of GloryBeauty Palace is to provide affordable, fashionable, and quality hair accessories that help individuals express their personality and boost confidence. The site will serve as both a catalog and online store, making it easy for customers to browse and purchase items.

3. Scenarios

  • A young professional wants a stylish hair clip to complement her office look and quickly finds options through the catalog.
  • A bride-to-be browses the site for elegant accessories to match her wedding dress and purchases directly online.

4. Color Scheme

Primary Color: Rose Gold (#B76E79) – used for headings, buttons, and accents.

Secondary Color: Soft Cream (#FFF5E1) – used for background areas to create a clean, elegant feel.

5. Typography

Font Family: Montserrat

  • Headings: Bold Montserrat for emphasis.
  • Body Text: Regular Montserrat for readability.

6. Wireframe

Mobile View:

  • Logo at top
  • Navigation menu (hamburger style)
  • Hero image/banner showcasing featured accessories
  • Product grid (2 columns)
  • Footer with contact info

Desktop/Wide View:

  • Logo top-left, navigation bar top-right
  • Large hero banner across the page
  • Product grid (4 columns)
  • Sidebar for filters (price, style, color)
  • Footer with links and contact info

7. Valid HTML and CSS

This site plan document uses valid HTML5 and CSS3, including semantic elements and a proper title. The selected color scheme and typography are applied consistently.
